# Legal Content

Terms for the website's published privacy, service, and marketing-consent documents.

## Language

**Effective version**:
The version whose effective date has arrived and is later than the effective dates of all other active versions of that document.

**Archived version**:
A previously effective document version retained unchanged so its exact content can be viewed and verified.

**Marketing consent wording**:
The exact optional opt-in statement shown to a person when they choose to receive marketing emails.
