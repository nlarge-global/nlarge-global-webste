# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are prospective freelance technology clients seeking a reliable partner for custom software, website improvements, maintenance and digital transformation. Government decision-makers are a later expansion audience and should receive a lightweight credibility signal with a route to a dedicated government transformation page.

## Product Purpose

NLarge Global presents its software development, platform improvement and long-term ownership capabilities, builds trust, and converts interested prospects into project inquiries.

## Positioning

NLarge is not positioned as a conventional delivery-only agency. Its meaningful difference is responsibility before, during and after implementation: understand, build, improve and take ownership.

## Operating Context

Prospects evaluate the company across desktop and mobile websites, then contact it for custom software, platform improvement, maintenance, government digital transformation or software products.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Core capabilities: build, improve and take ownership.
- Existing homepage must preserve the NLarge Global identity and working testimonial carousel.
- Government work should be represented modestly on the homepage and linked to a future dedicated page.
- Testimonials remain locally maintained; unapproved feedback must never publish automatically.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: NLarge Global.
- Core message: "We Build. We Improve. We Take Ownership."
- Existing visual assets include the NLarge logo and founder portraits.
- User interface should remain premium, professional, approachable and credible without appearing like a generic agency template.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing testimonial data is locally maintained.
- Founder profiles and portraits exist in the repository.
- The company email addresses are present in the current footer.
- No additional client metrics, case studies or government claims should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Responsibility and ownership distinguish the company from delivery-only agencies.
2. Clarity and evidence matter more than exaggerated claims.
3. Government credibility can build gradually without displacing the primary freelance client audience.
4. Keep the experience fast, accessible and easy to navigate on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website must remain keyboard usable, support reduced-motion preferences, provide visible focus states and use semantic headings and controls.
